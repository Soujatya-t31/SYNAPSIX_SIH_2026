Reporting Guidelines

01

Report only genuine public infrastructure issues — potholes, broken streetlights, garbage overflow, drainage, water leakage, damaged footpaths, faulty traffic signals and similar civic problems.

02

Add a clear photo whenever possible, taken close enough that the damage is visible, and allow location access so the report reaches the right department.

03

Keep descriptions factual and specific — mention nearby landmarks, how long the issue has persisted, and anything that makes it more dangerous (e.g. near a school or busy crossing).

04

Don't file duplicate reports for the same spot — JanDrishti automatically links nearby reports of the same issue into a single incident, and your report still counts.

05

Misuse of the platform — false reports, offensive content, or reporting private property disputes — may result in reports being removed.

About JanDrishti

JanDrishti is a citizen-first initiative that connects everyday people directly with the government departments responsible for fixing local infrastructure problems. Every report is verified, scored transparently and routed to the right department automatically — so the most dangerous, highest-impact problems surface first.

Privacy Policy

We collect only what's needed to route and resolve your report — category, description, an optional photo, and the location you share. Location data is used to route reports to the correct department and is shown publicly only as an approximate area, never your exact device location tied to your identity, unless you're logged in and viewing your own reports.

Terms of Service

JanDrishti is provided as a civic reporting tool. Reports should be accurate and made in good faith. Departments make final decisions on prioritisation and resolution timelines; the priority score shown is an assistive ranking, not a guarantee of response time.

Disclaimer

This is a prototype built for demonstration purposes. Response times, department assignments and resolution outcomes shown here are illustrative and may not reflect an official government commitment.
